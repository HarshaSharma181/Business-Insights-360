# 📊 Business Insights 360 – Power BI Dashboard

## 🚀 Project Overview

**Business Insights 360** is a comprehensive Power BI analytics solution designed to provide end-to-end visibility into business performance across **Finance, Sales, Marketing, Supply Chain, and Executive Operations**.

The dashboard enables stakeholders to monitor key business metrics, analyze trends, identify opportunities, and make data-driven decisions through interactive visualizations and dynamic reporting.

## 💰 Finance View

![Finance View](Images1/Finance%20View.jpg)

It helps stakeholders analyze profitability trends and understand customer and product contributions to overall financial performance.

### Key Insights

* Net Sales Performance
* Gross Margin Analysis
* Net Profit Tracking
* Profitability Trends
* Financial KPI Monitoring
* Customer & Product Performance Analysis

### Visualizations Used

* KPI Cards
* Trend Analysis Charts
* Financial Tables
* Performance Comparison Metrics
* Dynamic Filters & Slicers

### Business Value

Enables finance teams to monitor revenue, profitability, and overall financial performance for informed strategic decision-making.
